FAQ

Questions, answered.

Is Shelf Shelf free?

Yes. Everything you see on this page is free, with no ads and no account. If an optional Pro plan is ever offered, the core features stay free forever.

Extension, app or website — which should I use?

On a computer, the extension is the best fit: Shelf in every new tab, add the page you’re on, and reminders even when the browser is closed. The desktop app gives Shelf its own window and opens your sites in your default browser. On a phone, use the Android app or add the web app to your home screen.

Which browsers does the extension work in?

It’s on the Chrome Web Store, so it works in Chrome, Edge, Brave, Vivaldi, Opera and Arc. Firefox and Safari versions are on the way — the menu next to the Add button shows each store as it goes live.

Where are my apps and to-dos stored?

On your own device. The extension can also keep a copy in your browser account (Chrome Sync and similar) if you turn on Sync. Nothing is ever sent to us. Read the privacy policy.

Can I move my Shelf between the extension, the apps and the website?

Yes. In Settings choose Export Backup, then Import Backup on the other device. The same file works everywhere.

Windows or macOS says the app is from an unknown developer. Is it safe?

Yes — that message appears for new apps without a paid signing certificate. On Windows choose More info → Run anyway; on macOS open System Settings → Privacy & Security and click Open Anyway. You only do this once.

Why is there no App Store download for iPhone?

Apple only allows App Store installs. On iPhone and iPad, open Shelf Shelf in Safari and choose Share → Add to Home Screen — it opens full-screen and works offline.

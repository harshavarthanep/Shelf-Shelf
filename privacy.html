Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 22, 2026 · Applies to every version of Shelf Shelf (“Shelf”): the browser extension (Chrome, Edge, Firefox, Safari and others), the website and web app, the Windows, macOS and Linux apps, and the Android app.

The short version: Shelf has no accounts, no analytics and no ads. Your apps, folders and to-dos stay on your own device. We — the developer — never receive them.

What Shelf stores

The sites you save (address, name and logo), your folders and dock, your to-do lists (text, notes, dates, reminders and priority), the Quick Note and your settings. They are stored:

  • On your device. In the extension’s storage, in the browser’s IndexedDB for the website, or in the app’s own storage for the desktop and Android apps.
  • In your browser account, only if you turn on Sync Across Devices (extension only). Your browser (for example Chrome Sync or Firefox Sync) keeps this copy under its own privacy policy. Shelf never sees your account. Long Quick Notes stay on the device only.

Logos you upload and a wallpaper photo stay on the device where you added them. Shelf keeps your last ten versions on the device so you can restore one.

To time two one-off tips (pinning Shelf to the toolbar, and asking for a rating), Shelf keeps a few numbers on your device: the day it was installed, how many times it was opened, on how many days, and whether you answered. They never leave your device and are not synced.

What goes over the internet

  • Opening an app opens that website, exactly as clicking a link would. In the desktop and Android apps, sites open in your default browser.
  • Logos. To show a site’s logo, Shelf loads the icon from the site itself or from Google’s public favicon service (t2.gstatic.com, www.google.com/s2/favicons). That request contains the site’s address. Logos you upload are never sent anywhere.
  • Web search from the search box sends what you typed to the search engine you chose in Settings.
  • This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may record technical data such as your IP address when serving pages (see GitHub’s privacy statement). The download page asks GitHub for the latest version number and file sizes; app files download from GitHub.

Nothing is ever sent to the developer. Shelf contains no tracking, analytics or advertising code, and the extension does not load code from the internet.

Each version

Browser extension

Data lives in the extension’s storage on your computer, plus your browser account if Sync is on. Uninstalling the extension deletes its local data.

Website and web app (including “Add to Home Screen” on iPhone)

Data lives in your browser’s storage for this website. Shelf asks the browser to protect it from automatic clean-up. Clearing this site’s data in your browser deletes it — export a backup first.

Windows, macOS and Linux apps

Data lives in the app’s own folder on your computer. The app shows system notifications for your reminders. Uninstalling may keep your data so a reinstall picks it up; use Settings → Reset Everything to remove it.

Android app

Data lives in the app’s private storage on your phone. The app asks for permission to show notifications (for reminders). Export Backup saves a file to Documents/Shelf Shelf, and you choose whether to share it. Uninstalling the app deletes its data.

Shelf Shelf Pro (only if offered)

  • Payment happens on the Shelf Shelf website through Razorpay, under Razorpay’s privacy policy. Shelf never sees your card or UPI details.
  • The payment server keeps your email, the payment ID and your licence, so it can restore your purchase and meet tax and accounting rules.
  • The licence key is saved in your browser account so your other computers get Pro. It contains your email and the payment ID.
  • Free trial and automatic restore (Chrome and Edge, only if you allow it). When you start the trial or restore a purchase, the browser asks to let Shelf “know your email address”. Shelf then reads your browser account’s internal ID (not your email), scrambles it with SHA-256 (a one-way hash) and sends only that hash to the payment server, so it can remember that this account already had its trial and find a purchase made on it. You can turn this off anytime in your browser’s extension settings.

Your control

  • Export Backup gives you everything in one file. Reset Everything removes it from the device.
  • Turning off Sync stops the browser-account copy. Uninstalling the extension or the Android app deletes its local data.
  • For Pro purchase records, email us to ask for a copy or deletion. We reply within 30 days and keep only what tax law requires.
  • Shelf does not sell, share or transfer your data, and does not use it for advertising, credit decisions or any purpose other than being your launcher.

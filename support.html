Getting started

Add your first apps
  1. Tap + → New App and paste a link. The name and logo fill in by themselves.
  2. Or pick one of the suggestions, or Import Bookmarks (extension).
  3. In the extension you can also right-click any page → Add Page to Shelf.
Make folders and use the dock

In Edit mode, drag one app onto another to make a folder. Long-press or right-click an app to add it to the dock. Settings → Home Screen changes icon size, rows and sorting.

Add to-dos with reminders

Tap + → New To-Do and write it naturally, like “Pay rent tomorrow 9am !”. The time becomes a reminder and “!” marks it important.

Moving and backups

Move my Shelf to another device or browser
  1. On the old one: Settings → Export Backup.
  2. On the new one: Settings → Import Backup and pick the file.

The same file works in the extension, the website and every app. In the extension, Sync Across Devices also keeps your computers in step through your browser account.

I deleted something by mistake

Settings → Restore Previous Version keeps your last ten versions on the device.

Reminders

My reminders don’t ring
  • Extension: allow notifications when Shelf asks, and check your computer’s Focus / Do Not Disturb settings.
  • Website: reminders appear while Shelf is open. Use the extension or an app for reminders when it’s closed.
  • Android: allow notifications for Shelf Shelf in Android Settings → Apps → Shelf Shelf → Notifications, and turn off battery restrictions for it.
  • Desktop apps: allow notifications for Shelf Shelf in your system settings.

Installing the apps

Windows says “Windows protected your PC”

Click More info → Run anyway. This appears for new apps without a paid signing certificate; you only do it once.

macOS says it can’t check the developer

Open System Settings → Privacy & Security and click Open Anyway next to Shelf Shelf.

Android won’t install the APK

Allow your browser to install unknown apps when asked (Settings → Apps → your browser → Install unknown apps). To update, install the newer APK over the old one — your data stays.

Install on iPhone or iPad

Open the web app in Safari → Share → Add to Home Screen. It opens full-screen and works offline.

Your data

Delete all my data
  • In any version: Settings → Reset Everything.
  • Extension: remove it from your browser; turn off Sync to remove the account copy.
  • Website: clear this site’s data in your browser settings.
  • Android: uninstall the app. Desktop: Reset Everything, then uninstall.
  • Pro purchase records: email us and we’ll delete what the law allows.
